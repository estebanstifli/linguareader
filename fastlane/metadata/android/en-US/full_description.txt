Free bilingual books to learn languages, one story at a time.

Read in the language you are learning and keep the translation close by. LingoAudioBook brings books, audio and vocabulary together so you can enjoy a story while discovering how a new language works.

ONE STORY. TWO LANGUAGES.
Show the original text with a translation beneath each sentence, or read only the original and tap a word when you need help. Choose your reading language and translation language separately.

A FREE LIBRARY TO EXPLORE
Discover graded stories in English, Spanish and French: familiar fairy tales, classic adventures and adaptations for different reading levels. Choose a book that feels right for you and build a reading habit at your own pace.

READ IT. HEAR IT. UNDERSTAND IT.
Listen to the text with synthetic voices, adjust the speed and replay a sentence. Word highlighting and an optional animated finger help you follow along when word timing is available. Keep listening with the screen off.

WORDS THAT BELONG TO A STORY
See new vocabulary in context and save words to revisit later. Use bookmarks and pick up where you left off.

YOUR BOOKS, YOUR WAY
Import compatible EPUB and TXT books. Adjust text size and line spacing, and choose a light, dark or sepia theme for comfortable reading.

READY WHEN YOU ARE
Download books, translation models and the voices you need before reading offline. Translations are generated automatically; available languages and results depend on the selected language pair and voice.
