Learn a language the natural way: by reading stories you enjoy while you listen to them.

LingoAudioBook gives you graded readers for every level, a voice that reads aloud and highlights each word as it is spoken, and translation on tap. Everything works offline, with no account and no ads.

GRADED READERS
• Free libraries of books from A1 to B2 in English, Spanish and French, adapted from public-domain classics.
• Filter by level and start with the suggested book for your level.

READ AND LISTEN
• The voice reads the text and the current word lights up, so you connect sound and spelling.
• Repeat a sentence, skip ahead, change the speed and keep listening with the screen off.
• Choose your phone's voice or an optional offline neural voice.

TAP TO TRANSLATE
• Tap any word to see its translation in context, including phrasal verbs, contractions and expressions.
• See the translation of the whole sentence and save words to your vocabulary.
• On-device translation into your own language after a one-time download.

YOUR OWN BOOKS
• Open EPUB, PDF, Word, ODT, RTF, FB2, MOBI without DRM, TXT, Markdown, subtitles and web pages.
• Find the books you already keep in the folders you choose.

COMFORTABLE READING
• Light, dark and sepia themes, adjustable text size and line spacing.
• Bookmarks, search in the book, shelves and collections.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads and no tracking. Your books, progress and vocabulary stay on your phone.
