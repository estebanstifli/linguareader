Descripción
?
Turn every story into a language lesson.

LingoAudioBook brings reading and listening together. Hear a story, follow the highlighted words, and let an animated finger guide your eyes as you listen. Discover new vocabulary in context and enjoy learning at your own pace.

READ. LISTEN. FOLLOW ALONG.
Listen to books in English, Spanish, French and more. Word highlighting and an animated finger help you follow the text and connect written words with their pronunciation.

TRANSLATE YOUR WAY
Keep the original text on screen and tap a word to explore its meaning, or show translations beneath each sentence. Choose your reading and translation languages independently.

FIND YOUR NEXT STORY
Explore graded stories for different learning levels, from familiar fairy tales to classic adventures. Or bring your own reading material by importing EPUB and TXT books.

MAKE READING YOUR OWN
Adjust the voice, playback speed, text size and line spacing. Choose a light, dark or sepia theme, and switch the finger guide on or off whenever you like.

BUILD YOUR VOCABULARY
Save words you want to remember, bookmark your favourite passages and return to where you left off.

KEEP LISTENING
Continue listening with the screen off and use playback controls to pause, repeat or move between sentences.

Free to use, with no ads or in-app purchases.

Choose a story. Press play. Learn one word at a time.
