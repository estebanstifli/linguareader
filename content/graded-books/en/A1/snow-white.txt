Chapter 1: The Queen at the Window

It is winter. Snow falls from the sky. It falls on the big castle of the king.

In the castle, a queen sits at a window. The frame of the window is ebony, a very black wood.

The queen sews. She has a needle and a white cloth. She looks at the snow. It is very beautiful.

Then the needle goes into her finger. "Oh!" says the queen.

Three drops of blood fall from her finger. They fall on the white snow. The red on the white is very beautiful.

The queen looks at the red blood, the white snow and the black wood. She thinks, "I want a little girl. I want a girl with skin as white as snow. I want a girl with lips as red as blood. And I want a girl with hair as black as ebony."

Spring comes. Summer comes. Then winter comes again. And the queen has a baby girl.

The baby has skin as white as snow. She has lips as red as blood. She has hair as black as ebony.

"Her name is Snow White," says the queen. She holds the baby. She is very happy.

But the queen is very ill. She is weak. She cannot get up. She kisses the baby, and then she dies.

The king is very sad. He cries and cries.

One year goes by. The king takes a new wife. Now there is a new queen in the castle.

The new queen is very beautiful. But she is proud. She is cold in her heart. She does not love the king. She does not love the baby. She loves only one thing: her beautiful face.

The new queen has a magic mirror. It hangs on the wall in her room. And it can speak.

Every morning, the queen stands in front of the mirror. She looks at her face. She touches her hair. Then she says:

"Mirror, mirror, on the wall, who is the most beautiful of all?"

And the mirror always says:

"My queen, you are the most beautiful of all."

The queen smiles. She is happy, because the mirror always tells the truth. It cannot tell a lie.

Chapter 2: The Mirror Speaks

Years go by. Snow White grows.

Snow White is a kind girl. She helps the old gardener with the flowers. Everybody in the castle loves her.

And every year, she is more beautiful. Her skin is white like snow. Her lips are red like blood. Her hair is black like ebony.

Now Snow White is seven years old. One morning, the queen stands in front of the mirror. She touches her hair. She says:

"Mirror, mirror, on the wall, who is the most beautiful of all?"

The mirror is quiet for a moment. Then it says:

"My queen, you are beautiful, it is true. But Snow White is more beautiful than you."

The queen does not move. Her face is white. Then it is green. Then it is red.

"No!" she says. "No, no, no! That is not true!"

But the mirror cannot tell a lie. The queen knows it.

The queen is angry. She is very, very angry. Every day, her heart is colder and harder. It is like a stone.

One day, the queen calls a hunter. He has a bow and a long knife.

"Hunter," says the queen. "Take the girl into the forest. Take her far, far away."

"Yes, my queen," says the hunter.

"And then kill her," says the queen.

The hunter looks at the queen. "Kill her?" he says. "Kill Snow White? But she is only a child!"

"Do it," says the queen. "And bring me her heart. I want to see it. Then I know she is dead."

The hunter is afraid of the queen. Everybody is afraid of the queen.

"Yes, my queen," he says, very quietly.

The next morning, the hunter comes to Snow White.

"Come with me, little princess," he says. "Come into the forest. We can look at the flowers and the birds."

"Oh, yes!" says Snow White. "I love the forest!"

She takes his hand. She smiles at him. And they walk out of the castle and into the forest.

Chapter 3: Alone in the Forest

The forest is big and green. The trees are very tall. Birds sing in the branches.

Snow White and the hunter walk and walk. Snow White picks flowers. She is happy.

They walk deep into the forest. Here the trees are dark.

The hunter stops. He takes out his long knife.

Snow White looks at the knife. Then she looks at his face. "Hunter?" she says. "What is that knife for?"

"I am sorry, little princess," says the hunter. "The queen wants you dead."

Snow White cries. "Oh, please, dear hunter!" she says. "Please do not kill me! I can run into the forest. I can run far away. I never want to go home again. The queen never needs to see me again."

The hunter looks at her. She is so small. She is so beautiful. He cannot do it.

He puts the knife down. "Run, then, poor child," he says. "Run away. Run far into the forest. And never come back to the castle."

"Thank you!" says Snow White. "Thank you, dear hunter!"

She runs into the trees.

The hunter thinks, "Poor child. The wild animals can eat her."

Just then, a young wild pig runs out of the bushes. The hunter kills it with his knife. He takes its heart. He puts it in a little box.

He goes back to the castle. He gives the box to the queen.

"Here it is, my queen," he says. "The heart of Snow White."

The queen opens the box. She looks at the heart. She smiles a cold smile. "Good," she says. "Very good. Now I am the most beautiful of all."

In the forest, Snow White runs and runs. The thorns of the bushes cut her arms and her legs. She cries, but she does not stop.

Wild animals look at her from the trees, but they do not hurt her.

The sun goes down. It is evening. The forest is dark and cold.

Then Snow White sees a light. It is a little house in the trees.

"A house!" she says. "Maybe I can rest there."

She walks to the little house. She knocks on the door. Nobody answers. She opens the door and goes in.

Chapter 4: The Seven Little Men

Inside, everything is small. Everything is clean and nice.

In the middle of the room, there is a little table. On the table, there is a white cloth. And on the cloth, there are seven little plates. Next to each plate, there is a little spoon, a little knife and a little fork. And there are seven little cups.

Against the wall, there are seven little beds. Each bed has a white sheet.

Snow White is very hungry and thirsty. But she is a good girl. She does not want to take everything from one plate.

So she takes a little bread and soup from each plate. And she drinks one drop of wine from each cup.

Then she is very sleepy. She wants to lie down. She tries the beds. One is too long. One is too short.

Then she tries the seventh bed. It is just right. She lies down. She says a little prayer. And then she sleeps.

It is night. The door opens. Seven little men come in. They are dwarfs. They are very small, with long beards. They work all day in the mountains. They dig for gold and silver. Every night, they come home with seven little lamps.

The dwarfs light their lamps. They look around the room. Something is different.

The first dwarf says, "Who sits on my chair?"

The second dwarf says, "Who eats from my plate?"

The third dwarf says, "Who takes my bread?"

The fourth dwarf says, "Who eats my soup?"

The fifth dwarf says, "Who uses my fork?"

The sixth dwarf says, "Who cuts with my knife?"

The seventh dwarf says, "Who drinks from my cup?"

Then the first dwarf looks at his bed. "Somebody is in my bed!" he says.

Then the seventh dwarf looks at his bed. "Look!" he says. "Come and look!"

The seven dwarfs come with their lamps. They see Snow White. She is asleep.

"Oh!" they say. "Oh, look at her! She is so beautiful!"

They are very happy. They do not wake her up. They let her sleep in the little bed. The seventh dwarf sleeps one hour with each of his friends. Then the night is over.

In the morning, Snow White wakes up. She sees the seven dwarfs. She is afraid. But the dwarfs smile at her.

"What is your name?" they ask.

"My name is Snow White," she says.

"How do you come to our house?" they ask.

Snow White tells them everything: the queen, the hunter and the dark forest.

The dwarfs listen. Then the oldest dwarf says, "You can live here with us. You can cook and clean the house. You can make the beds. You can sew and wash. And we can keep you safe."

"Oh, yes!" says Snow White. "With all my heart!"

Chapter 5: The Old Woman with the Laces

Every morning, the dwarfs go to the mountains. They dig for gold and silver. Every evening, they come home. And every evening, there is hot soup on the table.

Snow White cooks and cleans. She makes the seven little beds. She washes the seven little cups. She is happy now.

The dwarfs love her very much. But every morning, they say the same thing.

"Snow White, be careful," says the oldest dwarf. "The queen can find you. Do not let anybody into the house!"

"Do not open the door!" say all the dwarfs.

"I promise," says Snow White.

In the castle, the queen stands in front of the mirror. She is happy. She thinks Snow White is dead. She smiles and says:

"Mirror, mirror, on the wall, who is the most beautiful of all?"

And the mirror says:

"My queen, you are beautiful here, it is true. But over the mountains, with the seven dwarfs, Snow White is a thousand times more beautiful than you."

The queen is cold with fear. Then she is hot with anger. "The hunter!" she says. "He lies to me! Snow White is not dead!"

She paints her face. She puts on old clothes. Now she looks like an old woman. Nobody knows her.

She takes a box of pretty laces. Then she walks over the seven mountains to the house of the dwarfs.

She knocks on the door. "Pretty things!" she says. "Pretty things for sale!"

Snow White looks out of the window. "Good morning, old woman," she says. "What do you have?"

"Nice things, pretty things," says the old woman. "Look! Laces of every colour!" She holds up a lace. It is pink and blue and yellow silk.

Snow White thinks, "This old woman is kind. She is not dangerous." She opens the door.

"Oh, child," says the old woman. "Your dress is not pretty. Come here. I can put a new lace in it for you."

Snow White stands in front of her. The old woman puts the lace into the dress. Then she pulls it. She pulls it fast and very hard.

Snow White cannot breathe. She falls to the floor. She does not move.

"Now I am the most beautiful again," says the queen. And she runs away.

In the evening, the dwarfs come home. They see Snow White on the floor. "Oh, no!" they cry.

They lift her up. They see the tight lace. They cut it with a knife. Snow White breathes a little. Then she breathes more. She opens her eyes.

"That old woman is the queen!" say the dwarfs. "Please be careful, Snow White! Do not open the door to anybody!"

Chapter 6: The Comb

The queen is back in the castle. She runs to her mirror. She says:

"Mirror, mirror, on the wall, who is the most beautiful of all?"

And the mirror says:

"My queen, you are beautiful here, it is true. But over the mountains, with the seven dwarfs, Snow White is a thousand times more beautiful than you."

"Again!" says the queen. "She is alive again!"

Her face is red with anger.

"This time, she must die," she says.

The queen knows magic. She goes down to a secret room under the castle. There are old books and green bottles.

She takes a pretty comb. It is silver, with little white pearls. She puts poison on it. Now the comb is beautiful, but it is very dangerous.

Then the queen puts on different clothes. Now she looks like a different old woman.

She walks over the seven mountains to the house of the dwarfs. She knocks on the door.

"Pretty things!" she says. "Pretty things for sale!"

Snow White looks out of the window. "Go away, please," she says. "I must not open the door to anybody."

"But you can look," says the old woman. "Look, child! You do not need to open the door. Look at this pretty comb!"

She holds up the comb. It shines in the sun.

Snow White loves the comb. It is so pretty.

"Only one look," she thinks. "Only one."

She opens the door.

"Ah," says the old woman. "Now let me comb your beautiful hair."

Snow White does not think. She stands still. The old woman puts the comb into her black hair.

The poison works fast. Snow White falls to the floor. She does not move.

The queen laughs. "Now you are dead," she says. "Now I am the most beautiful of all." And she runs away.

The sun goes down. The dwarfs walk home. They open the door. And they see Snow White on the floor.

"Not again!" they cry.

Then the youngest dwarf sees something in her hair.

"Look! A comb!" he says.

He pulls the comb out of her hair. Snow White opens her eyes. She sits up. "What happens?" she asks.

The dwarfs are very serious now. "Snow White," says the oldest dwarf. "That woman is the queen again. She wants you dead. Please, please, do not open the door. Do not take anything from anybody!"

"I promise," says Snow White. "This time, I really promise."

Chapter 7: The Apple

The queen is back in the castle. She runs to her mirror. Her hands shake. She says:

"Mirror, mirror, on the wall, who is the most beautiful of all?"

And the mirror says:

"My queen, you are beautiful here, it is true. But over the mountains, with the seven dwarfs, Snow White is a thousand times more beautiful than you."

The queen screams. "Snow White must die!" she says. "Even if I die too!"

She goes down to the secret room. She works all night. She makes a magic apple. It is a beautiful apple. One half is white, and one half is red. Only the red half has poison in it. Everybody who sees the apple wants to eat it.

Then she paints her face again. She puts on the clothes of a farmer's wife. She has a basket of apples.

She walks over the seven mountains. She knocks on the door of the little house.

Snow White looks out of the window. "I cannot open the door," she says. "I cannot let anybody in. The seven dwarfs say no."

"That is fine," says the farmer's wife. "I do not want to come in. I only want to sell my apples. Here, child. Take one. It is a present."

"No, thank you," says Snow White. "I must not take anything."

"Are you afraid of poison?" says the woman. She laughs. "Look. I can cut the apple in two. You eat the red half. I eat the white half."

She cuts the apple. She eats the white half. "Mmm," she says. "Very good."

Snow White looks at the red half. It is so red and beautiful.

Snow White puts her hand out of the window. She takes the red half. She bites it.

The apple is in her mouth. And Snow White falls to the floor.

The queen looks at her. She laughs a terrible laugh. "White as snow, red as blood, black as ebony!" she says. "This time, the dwarfs cannot wake you!"

She goes home to the castle. She runs to the mirror. "Mirror, mirror, on the wall, who is the most beautiful of all?"

And at last, the mirror says, "My queen, you are the most beautiful of all."

Her cold heart is happy now.

In the evening, the dwarfs come home. Snow White is on the floor. She does not breathe.

They look for laces and combs. They wash her face with water and wine. But nothing helps. Snow White does not wake up.

The seven dwarfs sit around her. They cry for three days.

"We must put her in the ground," says one dwarf.

"No," says the oldest dwarf. "Look at her. Her face is not white like death. Her lips are red. She looks like she is asleep. We cannot put her in the black ground."

So they make a box of glass. They put Snow White in it. They write her name on it in gold letters: Snow White, Daughter of a King.

Chapter 8: The Prince

The dwarfs take the glass box up the mountain. They put it on a rock. Every day, one dwarf sits next to it. He keeps Snow White safe.

Animals come to the glass box. They cry for Snow White. First comes an owl. Then comes a black bird, a raven. And then comes a little white bird, a dove.

Snow White lies in the glass box for a long, long time. But she does not change. Her skin is white as snow. Her lips are red as blood. Her hair is black as ebony. She looks like she is asleep.

One day, a prince comes through the forest. He is the son of a king from a far country. He comes to the house of the dwarfs. He wants a bed for the night.

In the morning, he goes up the mountain. And there, he sees the glass box. He sees Snow White in it. He reads the gold letters.

"She is so beautiful," he says. He turns to the dwarfs. "Please, give me this glass box. I can give you gold. I can give you anything you want."

"No," say the dwarfs. "We do not want gold. We do not want anything. We never give her away."

"Then give her to me as a present," says the prince. "I cannot live without her. I want to look at her every day. I promise to love her and keep her safe."

The dwarfs look at the prince. He has tears in his eyes.

"Yes," says the oldest dwarf at last. "You can take her."

The prince is very happy. He calls his servants. Four strong men lift the glass box. They carry it down the mountain.

But on the path, there is a bush. One servant does not see it. His foot hits the bush. He falls. The glass box shakes. It shakes very hard.

And the piece of apple comes out of Snow White's mouth!

Snow White opens her eyes. She lifts the lid of the box. She sits up. She is alive again!

"Oh!" she says. "Where am I?"

"You are with me," says the prince. He tells her everything.

"I love you more than anything in the world," he says. "Come with me to my father's castle. Be my wife."

Snow White looks at his kind eyes.

"Yes," she says.

The seven dwarfs cry. But this time, they cry because they are happy.

There is a big wedding at the castle of the prince. The seven dwarfs come in their best clothes.

The queen gets a letter. It says, "Come to the wedding of a young queen." She puts on her best dress. She stands in front of the mirror and says:

"Mirror, mirror, on the wall, who is the most beautiful of all?"

And the mirror says:

"My queen, you are beautiful here, it is true. But the young queen is a thousand times more beautiful than you."

The queen is angry. But she must see the young queen. She goes to the wedding.

She comes into the room. And there is the young queen. It is Snow White!

But the king knows everything. He has a pair of iron shoes. They are red hot from the fire. "You must put on these shoes," he says to the queen. "And you must dance."

The queen puts on the hot iron shoes. She dances and dances. She cannot stop. She dances and dances, and then she falls down. She never gets up again.

And Snow White and the prince live happily together for many, many years. The seven dwarfs visit them every summer.

The End
