The Quiet Garden

Chapter One

Anna opened the gate and walked into the garden. The grass was wet and the air smelled of rain. A small bird sat on the wall and looked at her.

"Good morning," she said. The bird did not answer, but it did not fly away either.

Chapter Two

In the afternoon the sun came out. Anna sat on the old bench and read her book. She read slowly, one sentence at a time, and she was happy.
